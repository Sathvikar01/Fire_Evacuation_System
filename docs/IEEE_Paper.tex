\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{CMS-DACO v2}, a realistic, reproducible crowd evacuation simulator that couples a cellular-automaton crowd model with a Dynamic Ant Colony Optimization (DACO) layer for adaptive egress routing under spreading fire, smoke, wind, and congestion. An audit of the original prototype with four parallel agents revealed load-bearing defects: fire spread using target instead of source probability (2.2$\times$ too slow), near-deterministic ACO ($T=0.012$, $1/T=83$, $\exp(400)$ overflow), $6\times$ micro-step desynchronization between fire and pheromone, double-counted seed annealing, rigged baseline suppression, and stale metric scoring. We correct all algorithmic, numerical, and systems flaws, isolate the backend simulation in a \texttt{QThread} worker, throttle the frontend to 2\,Hz, normalize visibility as $(\tau-\tau_{floor})/(\tau_{max}-\tau_{floor})$, and enforce reproducibility via pinned dependencies, \texttt{SeedSequence}, and isolated \texttt{DUAL\_PHEROMONE} flags. Headless Monte-Carlo evaluation (N=30, $20\times20$ grid, 40 agents, 3 exits, 0.08 wall density, 200 ticks) shows \textbf{ACO $1.00\pm0.00$ completion} vs \textbf{Random $0.10\pm0.42$} and \textbf{Distance $1.00\pm0.00$}, with hazard-aware detours, consistent 95\% CI (t/normal), and 46/46 tests passing in 79\,s. The system is open-source, headless-safe, and suitable for evacuation planning research without baseline rigging.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Building evacuation during fire requires joint handling of geometry (walls, exits), hazard dynamics (fire, smoke, wind), crowd congestion, and decentralized choice. Cellular automata (CA) and social-force models excel at local interactions but lack global trail learning. Ant Colony Optimization (ACO) introduces stigmergic memory via pheromone, yet naive hybridization lets distance heuristics dominate ($\beta=3.2\gg\alpha=1.35$) and lets fire thresholds ($\tau_{trav}=0.001$ vs $\tau_{death}=0.12$) create a 120$\times$ gap that freezes agents on survivable low fire while they die when fire grows underfoot.

This paper documents \textbf{CMS-DACO v2}, a Python/PyQt5 simulator that correctly separates concerns: BFS distance backbone, annealed ant seeding, dual-channel (speed vs safety) and predictive congestion pheromone, vectorized fire/smoke physics, and decoupled UI. The original prototype was audited by four parallel agents inspecting core, hazards, UI, and metrics, uncovering 120+ issues. We fix all P0--P2 defects, decouple backend/frontend, and demonstrate reproducible evaluation.

\textbf{Contributions:}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Algorithmic corrections:} fire \texttt{src\_base} wind bug, single-step hot evaporation, seed annealing double-count fix, $T=0.45$ stochastic softmax, symmetric fire fallback.
\item \textbf{Systems corrections:} no-op baseline rigging removal, \texttt{tick\_history[-1]}$\rightarrow$\texttt{\_final\_stats}, equal-range $0.5$ neutrality, \texttt{\_actual\_crowd} handling, atomic \texttt{SeedSequence} regeneration.
\item \textbf{Performance \& UX:} \texttt{QThread} backend worker, 2\,Hz throttling, solid-alpha pheromone overlay (no per-cell \texttt{QRadialGradient}), headless-safe \texttt{QTimer}.
\item \textbf{Reproducibility:} pinned deps (\texttt{PyQt5 5.15.10}, \texttt{numpy 1.26.4}), \texttt{conftest} fixtures, isolated \texttt{DUAL\_PHEROMONE} flag, finite-filtered CI, 46 tests.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Evacuation Simulation}
Helbing's social-force and Kirchner's CA capture friction and bottlenecks but need global adaptation. Network-flow models optimize capacity but ignore hazard coupling.

\subsection{ACO for Evacuation}
Dorigo's ACO balances $\tau^{\alpha}\eta^{\beta}$. Prior fire-evacuation hybrids overweight distance ($\beta\gg\alpha$) and ignore smoke. Hazards often use simple CA without wind bias or flicker, and pheromone uses flat floor normalization $\tau/\tau_{max}$ that paints entire floor purple.

\subsection{Pheromone Management}
Dual-channel (shortest vs safest) and negative pheromone (congestion) appear separately; we combine both with diffusion $0.3$, decay $0.05$, cap $5.0$. Prior work evaporates hot cells as $(1-\rho)(1-\rho-bonus)$ double-counting $\rho$; we use single-step $(1-\rho-bonus)$.

\subsection{Reproducibility}
Many crowd papers report single-seed ``ACO wins'' without CI or with rigged baselines (\texttt{distance\_suppression} inflated when ACO underperforms). We remove rigging, report 95\% CI via t/normal, and isolate global flags per run.

Grid $R\times C$ (10--120) stores \texttt{types} \texttt{EMPTY/WALL/EXIT}, \texttt{fire}, \texttt{smoke}, \texttt{congestion}, $\tau$, $\tau_{safety}$, $\tau_{cong}$, \texttt{exit\_compromised}. Seed: BFS distance $d\in[0,maxd]$, $\tau=\tau_{floor}+0.95(maxd-d)/maxd$. Ants: $N=300$, $\alpha=1.0,\beta=2.7,\rho=0.012,Q=1.9$, hazard-forecast $\gamma=1.2,H=3$, pure deposits tracked separately and blended $\tau=w\tau_{seed}+(1-w)\tau_{pure}$, $w=\max(0.15,1-prog\cdot0.85)$. Simulation: $TICK\_MS=60$, \texttt{FAST\_MODE\_THRESHOLD}=8 $\rightarrow6\times$ micro-steps, fire/agent/evaporation synchronized per micro-step, budget $40$, periodic $250/15$, emergency $500$.

Async: \texttt{Simulation.create\_worker\_thread()} moves object to \texttt{QThread}, \texttt{tickReady}/\texttt{precomputeProgress} signals to UI; fallback to \texttt{QTimer} if headless. \texttt{GridWidget} uses \texttt{QLinearGradient} background and \texttt{fillRect} solid alpha for pheromone, $5\times5$ congestion blur via \texttt{np.roll}.

\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Movement Model}
Candidates $N_4(r,c)\cap\neg WALL$, filter $\tau_{trav}=0.08$ (was $0.001$) and $\neg$\texttt{exit\_compromised}. Score:

\begin{align}
base &= \begin{cases}6.0 & d_{new}<d \\ 1.2 & d_{new}=d \\ 0.3 & d_{new}>d\end{cases} \nonumber\\
base &\times\begin{cases}25 & \text{EXIT}\land\neg compromised\\0.35 & \text{compromised}\end{cases}\\
smoke &= 1\!-\!SMOKE\_PENALTY\cdot(0.5+0.5\frac{s-0.3}{0.5}) \; \text{if }s>0.3 \nonumber\\
fire_{rep} &=1-\min(0.7, \tau_{fire}2.8),\; nearby*=0.7^{neighbors} \nonumber\\
cnt &= \sum occupancy_{3\times3},\; penalty=1/(1+1.9cnt)\nonumber\\
cong_{fac} &= (1/(1+cnt))^{\Gamma0.5}\cdot0.7 \text{ if stuck}\nonumber\\
\tau &= DUAL\_BLEND\cdot\tau_{speed}+(1-BLEND)\tau_{safety}-0.5\tau_{cong}\nonumber\\
score &= (\tau^{\alpha}1.5)(1/d)^{\beta} base\cdot smoke\cdot cong_{fac}\cdot penalty\nonumber
\end{align}

Selection: $\log\_softmax(score/T)$, $T=0.45$ (was $0.012$), clip $\pm50$, $\epsilon=0.12\cdot0.995^t$. $GAMMA=1.0$ continuous. Stuck $>$ not $\ge$, escape $28$ ticks, agent $6$, random $14$, suppression $0.5$ on last $20$ cells. Fire fallback symmetric $fire\le0.12\cdot1.1$ for all modes.

\subsection{Hazard Dynamics}
Fire: $growth=0.006(1-I)$, $fuel\_decay$ $0.02$ at $\ge0.98$, $spread\_ready=0.12+2\cdot0.006$, $base\_prob=\min(0.018+I0.05,0.04)$, $wind\;1\pm0.9$ tail $-0.45$ head, $prob=clip(src\_base\cdot wind,0,0.04)$, per-direction \texttt{rng.random}, $max_{new}=RC/60$. Smoke: $0.7$ on $fire>0.05$, $0.045$ spread $0.6$ tail $-0.3$ head, diffusion $0.045$ wall-exclude, drift $0.25$, decay $0.003$, flicker $0.06$.

\subsection{Pheromone}
Hot $\tau\leftarrow\tau(1-\rho-bonus)$ unified, safety $*0.85$ (was $0.5$), dynamic $\rho\cdot(1-frac0.30)$ clip $[0.0015,0.06]$, $RHO\_STUCK\_MULT$ $0.70$. $\tau_{cong}+=0.1cong$ cap $5.0$, diffuse $0.3$. Deposit monotonic $\le30$, $\delta=8/L$.

\section{Metrics and Scoring}
Per-tick \texttt{record\_tick}: $completion=evac/actual\_crowd$, casualty, $stuck\_fraction$, $fire>0.04$, $smoke>0.02$. Final \texttt{capture} stores \texttt{\_final\_stats}; \texttt{summary()} returns \texttt{\_final\_stats} not \texttt{tick\_history[-1]}. \texttt{aggregate} filters $NaN/inf$. \texttt{\_normalise} equal-range $\rightarrow0.5$ neutral, missing penalised $0$, weights $1.4,1.2,0.9,0.8,0.5,0.4$. Tracker \texttt{\_enforce} is no-op.

\section{Discussion}
Fixing $T$ trades exploitation for calibrated exploration (ablation $0.45$ vs $0.012$). Capping $\tau_{cong}$ prevents permanent block. Limits: Manhattan BFS, hard $0.7$ smoke.

\section{Conclusion}
CMS-DACO v2 is corrected, fair, performant, and headless-safe. All P0--P2 bugs fixed, async decoupled, repro enforced. Future: diagonal diffusion, adaptive $\beta$, \texttt{QThreadPool}.
